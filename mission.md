# Mission - Nagorik AI

*This document outlines the core vision and mission of Nagorik AI, our submission for the Gemini API Developer Hackathon on Devpost.*
## Vision

Build the world's most accessible AI-powered citizen platform that enables every person in Bangladesh to access any government service through natural conversation in Bangla and English.

Nagorik AI should become the single digital entry point for citizens, businesses, and government services.

A citizen should not need to know:

* Which ministry owns a service
* Which website to visit
* Which form to download
* Which office to contact
* Which process to follow

They should simply ask:

"How do I renew my passport?"

or

"My NID is lost. What should I do?"

and receive accurate, official, step-by-step guidance instantly.

---

## Mission

To simplify access to every government service in Bangladesh through a trusted AI assistant powered entirely by verified government data and official service workflows.

Nagorik AI will:

* Explain government services in simple language
* Guide citizens through application processes
* Calculate fees and requirements
* Generate personalized checklists
* Answer questions using official sources only
* Reduce confusion, travel, waiting time, and misinformation

---

## Problem

Government services are fragmented across hundreds of websites, offices, portals, and agencies.

Citizens often struggle to:

* Find correct information
* Understand requirements
* Complete forms correctly
* Track application status
* Know where to go next

This creates:

* Long processing times
* Unnecessary visits
* High support burden
* Frustration and misinformation

---

## Solution

Nagorik AI acts as a unified AI layer across all government services.

The platform will:

1. Understand citizen intent
2. Retrieve information from verified sources
3. Generate accurate guidance
4. Provide personalized next steps
5. Connect citizens to official systems

Instead of searching multiple websites, citizens interact with one assistant.

---

## Core Principles

### Official Sources Only

Every answer must originate from:

* Government websites
* Government APIs
* Official regulations
* Official circulars
* Approved datasets

No hallucinated information.

---

### Citizen First

Design every workflow around citizens, not agencies.

Success is measured by:

* Time saved
* Confusion reduced
* Tasks completed

---

### Trust and Transparency

Every answer must show:

* Source
* Last update date
* Responsible organization

Citizens should always know where information comes from.

---

### Accessibility

Nagorik AI must work for:

* Urban citizens
* Rural citizens
* Elderly users
* Low-literacy users
* Mobile-first users
* Persons with disabilities

---

### Multilingual

Support:

* Bangla
* English

Future:

* Regional dialect support
* Voice interaction

---

## Long-Term Goal

Create a National AI Citizen Assistant capable of supporting every major government service category:

* Identity and NID
* Birth and death registration
* Passports and visas
* Tax and finance
* Education
* Healthcare
* Land and property
* Utilities
* Social welfare
* Transportation
* Licensing and permits
* Business registration
* Public complaints
* Local government services

---

## MVP Goal

Launch within 90 days with a small set of high-demand services and validate:

* Citizen adoption
* Answer accuracy
* Time savings
* User satisfaction

The MVP is not the final product.

The MVP proves the model.

---

## Success Definition

Nagorik AI succeeds when a citizen can complete a government task faster, more confidently, and with less effort than using traditional government channels.

The ultimate goal is simple:

"One conversation. Any government service."
